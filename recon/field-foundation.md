### R1.2 — Bus semantics
Answer: `EventSerialBus` is a callback-based publish/subscribe router. `publish` snapshots handlers under a mutex, then launches one coroutine per handler on the injected `CoroutineScope` (asynchronous, dispatched). Delivery order across handlers and across events is not guaranteed. The bus has no buffering, backpressure, or replay. `EventSerialBus.emissions(runId)` is a per-collector `Flow<Emission>` adapter with an explicit `DROP_OLDEST` channel. Historical replay exists one layer up as `EventRelayService.replayEvents(fromTime, toTime)` reading the `EventStore` table. No other bus type exists.
